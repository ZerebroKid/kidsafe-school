No coins wallet, no redeem-with-coins flow, no leaderboard — rewards unlock purely by finishing today's mission and stay open until midnight. Locked rewards always show progress, never a dead end. Parent-controlled Focus Mode is firm but never humiliating: no shaming copy, an always-visible emergency exit on the self-controlled state, and "message my parent" on the locked one. Before launch: the three-price comparison needs signed partners (fallback screen ships otherwise); the Exam Predictor keeps its Coming-soon ribbon until live; reward imagery still needs licensed photography.
