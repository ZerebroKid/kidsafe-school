Junior · handoff notes Flow: Home is the launch screen. Tapping a tile opens Fun / Cartoons / Reels / Games; back arrow returns Home. There is no bottom nav in Junior — every section is opened from a Home tile, and the back arrow at the top-left returns to Home. That keeps one single, obvious way to navigate for a 4-year-old. Fun: Zumi (the cat from Home, not the round robot) bobs, speaks, and offers three big taps — kahani, gaana, sawaal — plus a press-and-hold mic. All voice; nothing to read. Cartoons and Reels are shells for YOUR content — hero player + 2-up grid for cartoons, full-bleed 9:16 feed with like / share / sound for reels. Striped slots mark exactly where your files drop in. Games (rebuilt to your reference): dark arcade hero with a glowing lock, "Saare games kholo", the yellow ₹300 CTA and Safe / No Ads / 100+ Games trust row; then Boys ke games and Girls ke games as two horizontally-scrolling rails of 10 titles each with See All; then the purple one-time-payment banner. Licensing — important: the cartoon and battle-royale artwork is cropped from your AI reference and shows third-party characters (Doraemon, Shinchan, Chhota Bheem, a PUBG-style soldier). These cannot ship. Replace them with your own cartoon stills and your own game art in the same slots. Also note the bottom fifth of the four Home tile images carries a vertical smear — that is where the reference’s baked-in title text was painted out, not intended art; your real tile artwork replaces it. The girls' section is already drawn as labelled placeholders for that reason. Open question: this junior app keeps coins and hearts (3/10) from your reference, while the senior app dropped XP/coins.
